Rocket

Rocket Pro Navigate

I lead product design across a B2B mortgage platform used by bankers and real estate professionals. The work spans core workflows, platform modernization and emerging AI experiences.

AVP, Product Design2023 to present
The complexity

Mortgage work has a lot of roles, handoffs and decisions happening at once. My job is to help make those workflows feel coherent instead of exposing all of that complexity to the person using the product.

What I lead

I lead design direction across core banker and realtor workflows, portal redesign and AI agent experiences, partnering closely with product, engineering and business teams.

What I care about

I’m always looking for the clearest path through the system: fewer dead ends, stronger hierarchy, better consistency and AI that actually helps move the work forward.

MY ROLEBring structure to a complicated B2B platform.

I help teams align around the experience, connect related workflows and make the product feel like one system instead of a collection of features.

AI + PRODUCTUseful before impressive.

I’m interested in AI when it reduces effort, surfaces the right information or helps someone take the next action—not when it simply adds another layer to learn.
PayPal

Merchant checkout

I designed merchant payment, checkout and account experiences in a space where even small moments of confusion can quickly become a trust problem.

Sr. Product Designer, Merchant2022 to 2023
The complexity

Checkout has to balance merchant needs, customer confidence, payment rules and a lot of edge cases without making any of that feel heavy.

What I worked on

I worked across merchant payment, checkout and account experiences, using shared patterns and design-system thinking to make high-trust workflows clearer and more consistent.

What I cared about

Reducing friction without reducing confidence—clear choices, understandable states and a path forward that feels obvious when money is involved.

DESIGN PRINCIPLETrust is part of the interface.

Payment experiences have to explain enough to build confidence while staying out of the way of the task someone came to complete.

SYSTEMS THINKINGConsistency lowers the cognitive load.

I look for patterns that can work across related flows so merchants don’t have to relearn the product every time the context changes.
YPO

Global member platform

I designed across YPO’s web and mobile member experience, including discovery, events, networking and content for a global community.

UX Designer2017 to 2022
The complexity

A global membership platform has a lot of different reasons to exist at once. The challenge was helping members move naturally between people, events, content and the things happening around them.

What I worked on

I designed member discovery, events, networking and content experiences across web and mobile, thinking about how those parts connected rather than treating them as separate destinations.

What I cared about

Making a large ecosystem feel personal. The platform needed to help a member find the right person, event or opportunity without feeling like they were searching through an organization chart.

CONNECTED EXPERIENCEPeople, places and content should feel like one product.

I focused on helping members move between discovery, events and networking without losing context along the way.

GLOBAL PRODUCTUseful at a global scale. Personal at the individual level.

The best member experience still has to feel relevant to the person opening the app right now.
Scouting America

Member + volunteer tools

I worked in UX content and service design across training, account and program workflows used by members and volunteers.

UX Writer2012 to 2017
The complexity

Volunteer-driven systems have to work for people with very different levels of experience, time and technical confidence. The product can’t assume everyone already knows how the organization works.

What I worked on

I worked across training, account and program workflows, using UX content and service-design thinking to make instructions, decisions and next steps easier to understand.

What I cared about

Good content is product design. Clear language, useful structure and the right information at the right moment can remove as much friction as a redesigned screen.

CONTENT AS DESIGNWords are part of the workflow.

I use content to reduce uncertainty, explain what matters and make the next action easier to understand—especially in systems people may only use occasionally.

SERVICE UXDesign for the person who doesn’t know the system.

When a product supports volunteers, the experience has to help people succeed without requiring them to become experts in the organization first.
