Brown Books Publicity & PR Services

Brown Books offers three publicity and marketing programs to build an author's platform, generate awareness, and support a book's path to market. From prepublication social media marketing to standardized publicity services, and fully customized campaigns, each program is designed to help authors build visibility and connect with readers.

1. Standard Publicity Services

For projects with publicity budgets of $10,000 or less

Standard Publicity Services provide a structured publicity offering for authors who need professional support generating awareness for their books.

Services may include

  • Press release writing and distribution
  • Author biography and book positioning
  • Basic press kit development
  • Targeted media list research
  • Outreach to journalists, editors, and relevant publications
  • Podcast and radio outreach
  • Media follow-up and response coordination
  • Publicity calendar and launch coordination
  • Coverage monitoring and reporting

2. Custom Publicity Campaigns

Starting at $15,000 · $200 per hour

Custom Publicity Campaigns are developed around the book's subject, target audience, author platform, and market potential. Each campaign combines strategic planning with targeted outreach and execution, allowing Brown Books to build a publicity program suited to the individual title.

Services may include

  • Publicity strategy and book positioning
  • Media research and targeted outreach
  • Press release and press kit development
  • Regional and national media relations
  • Book reviews, interviews, and feature stories
  • Podcast, radio, and broadcast bookings
  • Author appearances, speaking opportunities, and events
  • Industry, expert, and influencer outreach
  • Launch coordination and promotional support
  • Campaign monitoring, follow-up, and reporting

3. Social Media Marketing

Designed to begin before publication and continue through launch and beyond

Social Media Marketing helps authors establish a recognizable presence, build an audience, and generate interest in their books before publication. By beginning early, authors can build momentum around the book's development, cover reveal, preorder period, publication date, and ongoing availability.

This service also creates a natural foundation for more extensive publicity campaigns.

Services may include

Author and book brand development: Establish a consistent voice, message, and visual direction.

Platform strategy: Identify the most relevant platforms for the author and book, such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or TikTok.

Content planning: Build a calendar around key milestones, including manuscript development, cover reveal, preorders, publication, and launch.

Content creation: Develop social posts, graphics, book quotes, excerpts, author features, and behind-the-scenes content.

Audience development: Help grow an engaged audience around the author, book, and subject matter.

Preorder and launch promotion: Build awareness and direct interested readers toward purchasing opportunities.

Ongoing book promotion: Maintain visibility after publication through continued content and promotional campaigns.

Performance reporting: Review engagement and other relevant metrics to inform future content.
