Social Media Launch Support

Pairs with either publicity program

Social Media Marketing helps authors establish a recognizable presence, build an audience, and generate interest in their books before publication. By beginning early, authors can build momentum around the book's development, cover reveal, preorder period, publication date, and ongoing availability.

This service also creates a natural foundation for more extensive publicity campaigns.

Services may include

  • Author and book brand development: Establish a consistent voice, message, and visual direction.
  • Platform strategy: Identify the most relevant platforms for the author and book, such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or TikTok.
  • Content planning: Build a calendar around key milestones, including manuscript development, cover reveal, preorders, publication, and launch.
  • Content creation: Develop social posts, graphics, book quotes, excerpts, author features, and behind-the-scenes content.
  • Audience development: Help grow an engaged audience around the author, book, and subject matter.
  • Preorder and launch promotion: Build awareness and direct interested readers toward purchasing opportunities.
  • Ongoing book promotion: Maintain visibility after publication through continued content and promotional campaigns.
  • Performance reporting: Review engagement and other relevant metrics to inform future content.
